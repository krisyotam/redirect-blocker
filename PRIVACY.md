# Privacy policy

Redirect Blocker processes browsing data only to provide its redirect-blocking features.

- Redirect history, protected sites, allowlisted domains, and blocked domains are stored locally in the browser through `chrome.storage.local`.
- No analytics, telemetry, advertisements, remote APIs, or external servers are used.
- No browsing data is sold, shared, or transmitted by the extension.
- Export files are created only when the user clicks an export button and are saved through the browser's normal download flow.
- Removing the extension removes its browser-managed local data. History can also be cleared at any time from the extension dashboard.

The extension source is available for inspection under the MIT License.
